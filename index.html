AI Money Rings: who funds whom, and where to watch

AI demand is real, and it is being monetised: the frontier labs are growing faster than any software company in history, and demand for computing capacity still exceeds supply. Staying invested in this cycle means understanding how it is financed. The build-out is increasingly funded by the companies that sell to it: Nvidia invests in its own customers, Broadcom lends to the lab that leases its chips, and roughly half of the cloud providers’ backlog is owed by just two AI labs. Super-cycles rarely end because valuations look stretched; they end when financing dries up. This map shows where that risk sits, and which companies stand on firmer ground.
